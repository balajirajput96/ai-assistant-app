# Play Store Listing Draft

## App name

Atlas

## Short description

A careful AI workspace for chat, voice, task planning and reviewable automations.

## Full description

Atlas helps you turn questions, notes and priorities into clear drafts and plans. It keeps your task status visible, stores history locally by default, and asks for review before any future connection could affect an external service.

Use Atlas to draft, summarize, plan and organize your next steps. You can speak a short request, review a transcription, and listen to assistant responses. Low-risk templates help you prepare repeatable work without hidden background activity.

Atlas does not claim to perform external actions unless a clearly identified connection and approval flow are configured. You stay in control of what is connected, what data is shared, and what is retained.
