# Community Feedback

## Method

Community content is used only as product-design evidence, not as a security standard or a substitute for official documentation. The selected public discussion is narrow in sample size and should not be generalized as market research.

## Observed theme

The discussion reviewed emphasizes four user expectations: data should not leave the app without explicit permission; accidental exposure of passwords or internal records should be mitigated; retention should be understandable; and deletion should be available and verifiable. [1]

## Product response

The design includes user-visible connector scopes, confirmation before external actions, local memory/history deletion, a privacy screen, data-minimizing defaults and plain-language task logs. These choices are also consistent with Google Play’s requirements to disclose data practices and limit sensitive-data handling to expected app functionality. [2]
